The collected information describes a one-time purchase for the Early Access game, not a subscription. It does not establish the exact launch-day content count, future downloadable content policy, soundtrack inclusion, deluxe edition, or regional package structure. The free demo is the lowest-risk way to judge the current combat, controls, art direction, and basic premise before purchase. ## Why can the amount differ by region?

Steam can display a local currency, regional price, taxes, and any active promotion at checkout. A US-dollar base-price statement cannot be converted with a live exchange rate to predict the exact amount another player will pay. Currency conversion, regional pricing policy, and tax handling are different inputs.

For that reason, screenshots from another account or territory are context, not a personal quote. The reliable number is the amount shown on the official Emberville store page for your logged-in Steam region at the time of purchase. If a promotion is active, also check its end time before assuming the lower amount is permanent.

Gift copies and keys can have region restrictions. A lower listing elsewhere may not activate in your country, may not have the same refund protection, or may not come from an authorized seller. This guide does not validate third-party key offers.

## How should you decide whether Early Access is worth it?

First decide whether the available single-player Early Access scope is enough without relying on future co-op, console ports, or a completed story. Planned features can be meaningful reasons to follow development, but they should not be counted as delivered value. If a future feature is essential, waiting for it is the clearer choice.

Second, use the demo on the computer and input method you intend to use. A satisfying demo does not guarantee long-term depth, yet it provides better personal evidence than a trailer alone. Review the current requirements and the [Steam Deck status](/guides/steam-deck/) separately if handheld play is important.

Third, read the launch notes and refund terms available through the store. This wiki can explain the scope and track official statements, but it cannot promise eligibility in an individual purchase case. Keep the receipt and do not rely on a community post as a substitute for the policy shown during checkout.
