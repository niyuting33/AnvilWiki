Emberville has a broad plan for console releases around version 1.0, but **no specific console is confirmed**. PS5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, and Switch 2 currently have no announced product page or release date.

## Which Emberville platforms are confirmed?

Windows PC is the confirmed Early Access platform. The January 7, 2027 target applies to that PC milestone, not to a simultaneous console launch. Steam Deck uses the PC build and is tracked separately from native console ports.

| Platform          | Current status       |
| ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Windows PC        | Early Access planned |
| PlayStation 5     | Not announced        |
| Xbox              | Not announced        |
| Nintendo Switch   | Not announced        |
| Nintendo Switch 2 | Not announced        |

<Callout type="warn" title="A general plan is not a platform announcement">
  Do not turn “consoles planned for 1.0” into a confirmed list of devices.
</Callout>

## Why can no console date be estimated?

Version 1.0 itself has no confirmed date. A flexible Early Access development estimate cannot be added to January 7 to manufacture a console window. Certification, performance targets, platform agreements, and porting work can also differ for each device.

Follow the [release-date page](/guides/release-date/) for the official PC milestone and the [Steam Deck guide](/guides/steam-deck/) for handheld PC status. The [multiplayer page](/guides/multiplayer/) explains why a later console plan does not automatically confirm cross-play.

## What would count as a real console confirmation?

A reliable announcement should name the exact platform and come from Cygnus Cross, the platform holder, or an official store page. Generic database entries, search suggestions, price-comparison listings, and player requests do not confirm a port.

<Accordion title="Would a Switch announcement also confirm Switch 2?">
  No. Each console edition requires explicit naming. Compatibility between systems should not be
  assumed from one announcement.
</Accordion>

## What does the version 1.0 console plan actually tell us?

It indicates that consoles are part of the broader direction, not that every major console has a scheduled port. A roadmap can express intent before platform agreements, technical targets, certification, store pages, and timing are ready. It should be reported as a general plan and nothing more.

Version 1.0 has no confirmed date. Adding a guessed porting period would create a date from two unknowns. Even after a PC 1.0 announcement, console versions could be simultaneous, later, staggered, or changed; only a platform-specific announcement resolves that.

## Why are storefront entries important?

An official platform store page connects a product to a device, publisher, region, and release state. It can provide languages, player modes, editions, ratings, and a wishlist or purchase action. No such console listing is confirmed in the collected material for Emberville.

Third-party database placeholders are weaker evidence because they may be generated from search demand or repeat a rumor. A retailer result without a platform-holder page is not confirmation. “We would like to” is also not the same as “announced for PS5.”

## What about controls, performance, and cross-save?

None can be derived from the PC version alone. Console controls may require interface changes, and performance targets can vary by hardware mode. A port may have separate save storage, accounts, achievements, or update timing.

Cross-save requires an explicit progression-transfer system. Cross-play requires compatible networks and multiplayer on the relevant builds. Since initial Early Access is single-player, neither capability should be assumed from the console plan.

<Callout type="warn" title="Platform plans do not bundle features">
  A console announcement would still need separate confirmation for cross-play, cross-save, local
  co-op, online co-op, achievements, and performance modes.
</Callout>

## How should console players follow development?

Check official Cygnus Cross and Emberville channels for a post that names the exact device. The [community directory](/guides/community/) lists those sources. When a platform is announced, record whether it concerns Early Access or version 1.0 and whether a store page exists.

Keep region and edition details separate until the store provides them. A logo in an event montage should be checked against accompanying official text. Players considering Steam Deck should use the [Steam Deck guide](/guides/steam-deck/) because it evaluates the PC build under SteamOS, not a native console port.

## What would make this page change?

This page will mark a console confirmed only when Cygnus Cross, the platform holder, or an official store names it. A release date needs the same attribution. Performance modes, physical editions, subscription availability, and regions remain **[待补充]** until their own evidence appears.

If an announcement is delayed or cancelled, the newest official statement becomes current while the prior one remains historical. The goal is to answer what players can actually access, not preserve an optimistic platform list after facts change.

## Why should each console have its own evidence row?

PlayStation 5, Xbox Series systems, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2 are different products with separate technical and commercial decisions. Mentioning one in a question does not transfer evidence to another. Even systems in the same family may have different performance modes, store availability, or compatibility rules.

This page will therefore update the platform table one row at a time. A confirmed PS5 page would change only the PlayStation row unless the announcement names other systems. That prevents a headline such as “coming to consoles” from silently becoming a list the developer never published.

## Can PC requirements predict console performance?

No. PC minimum specifications describe a supported Windows configuration, not a resolution or frame-rate promise for fixed hardware. Console builds can use platform-specific optimization, graphics modes, control changes, and certification requirements. Until a native build is announced and tested, performance is **[待补充]**.

The same caution applies to physical editions and subscription catalogs. A planned digital port does not confirm a disc, cartridge, Game Pass, PlayStation Plus, or other service. Each would need a current store or platform announcement.
