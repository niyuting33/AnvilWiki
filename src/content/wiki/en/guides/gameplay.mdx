Emberville combines action-RPG combat inside the Vitromotus with resident rescue, gathering, crafting, and town reconstruction. Progress in the dungeon supports the settlement, and restored services prepare the player for deeper exploration.

## How does the core gameplay loop work?

Players enter dangerous areas, fight creatures, gather resources, find equipment, and rescue survivors. Returning to town lets those resources and residents contribute to rebuilding, production, and new services. The two halves are connected rather than functioning as separate game modes.

| Gameplay pillar        | Confirmed role                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Vitromotus exploration | Combat, resources, residents, and progression |
| Town rebuilding        | Restores services and supports future runs    |
| Combat                 | Melee, ranged, and magic approaches           |
| Life systems           | Gathering, crafting, farming, and cooking     |
| Estate                 | Functional and decorative personal space      |

## Is Emberville a roguelike or a seasonal game?

The safest genre description is pixel-art action RPG with dungeon exploration and town building. Changing dungeon layouts or repeatable runs do not alone establish every traditional roguelike rule. No confirmed seasonal content model is available.

## What makes the dungeon and town halves connected?

Dungeon exploration is presented as the dangerous source of encounters, discoveries, resources, and rescued residents. Town rebuilding provides a place where those results can support facilities, crafting, farming, cooking, and later preparation. The two halves are not separate concepts; progress outside combat is intended to support another trip into the Vitromotus.

That relationship should be described without inventing recipes or upgrade trees. A resident may be associated with a town role in official material, but the exact unlock order, costs, and benefits need the live build. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) recommends following current objective text rather than an old fixed route.

The changing nature of the Vitromotus is part of the premise, yet the evidence does not justify a strict procedural formula. Room order, reset rules, persistence, and difficulty scaling remain **[待补充]**. Calling Emberville a conventional roguelike would imply mechanics that are not confirmed.

## How should combat be evaluated?

Start with movement, attack direction, commitment, recovery, and dodge timing. A weapon's feel is more than displayed damage: reach, animation, positioning, resource use, and class interaction affect a fight. Compare one change at a time so equipment, class, and execution are not confused.

Official material supports melee, ranged, and magical approaches at a high level. It does not establish a final best weapon, class ranking, or permanent damage formula. Any ranking before a tested launch roster should remain a draft or mark unsupported entries **[待补充]**.

Boss observations require the same discipline. Spider Matriarch is supported as a demo encounter, while exact health, phases, damage, and drops are not confirmed here. Use the [Bosses section](/bosses/) for evidence-limited strategies.
