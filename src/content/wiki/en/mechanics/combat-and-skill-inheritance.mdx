## How does Emberville combat work?

Emberville is an action RPG whose official description emphasizes positioning, timing, and knowledge. Weapons provide bound combos, while combat classes add active and passive abilities. Melee, magic, and ranged fighting are all planned for the Early Access experience.

<Callout type="info" title="Confirmed system, incomplete formulas">
The combat pillars are official, but public sources do not yet provide stable damage formulas, invulnerability timing, stagger thresholds, or final balance values.
</Callout>

## Why do weapon combos matter?

The weapon in hand determines unique combo options, so changing weapons can alter basic combat behavior even before class skills are considered. This makes weapon category a separate build layer rather than a cosmetic stat stick. The exact combo lists and scaling rules are **[待补充]**.

Players should learn movement and attack timing before optimizing numbers. The [gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay/) covers the broader loop, while the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) organizes the early priorities that can be confirmed without inventing routes or rewards.

## How does skill inheritance change a build?

Official Steam material says combat classes can inherit passive and active skills from other learned classes. This supports hybrid fighting styles and gives class progression value beyond the currently active class. Public material does not state the final number of inherited slots or whether every ability can transfer.

The [class-system guide](/classes/class-system/) separates confirmed switching rules from unknown restrictions. The [skills directory](/skills/) lists demo-observed abilities by class and evidence status, but it intentionally avoids using pre-release numbers for rankings.

## What should players verify after an update?

Check whether skill names, weapon behavior, inheritance restrictions, costs, cooldowns, and rank caps changed. A patch can alter a build even when its headline class identity remains the same. Pages on this site show a game-version label and verification date so older demo observations are easier to spot.
