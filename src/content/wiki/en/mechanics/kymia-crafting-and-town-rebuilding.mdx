## What is Kymia in Emberville?

Kymia is described as a mysterious energy born from emotions inside the Vitromotus. In the story, it contributes to the strange persistence of life and death within the prison. In demo-observed combat, Kymia also functions as a resource associated with class abilities, especially Mage skills.

The exact regeneration formula, reservation rules, default capacity, and interactions with equipment remain **[待补充]**. Browse the [skills directory](/skills/) to see which observed abilities mention Kymia without treating old numerical values as final.

## How are gathering and crafting connected to progression?

The official Early Access description includes gathering, crafting, farming, cooking, equipment, and skills. Rescued townspeople teach trades and help restore Emberville, so production systems are part of progression rather than an isolated side activity. Players can gather materials to create equipment, potions, food, tools, and trinkets.

## What does rebuilding Emberville unlock?

The player rescues survivors and helps rebuild their ruined town. Official material says residents offer rewards, teachings, quests, trades, stories, and mechanic unlocks as the town returns to life. The precise building order and required materials are **[待补充]**.

Rebuilding therefore acts as a bridge between exploration and new capabilities. The [gameplay guide](/guides/gameplay/) covers the known pillars, and the [tips guide](/guides/tips/) keeps uncertain routes and rewards clearly marked.

## What is the Estate?

The Estate is the player's customizable space in Emberville. Official material says it can hold buildings, furniture, plants, and decorations, while supporting production needed for journeys into the Vitromotus. Exact plot limits, placement rules, storage behavior, and unlock timing remain unconfirmed.
